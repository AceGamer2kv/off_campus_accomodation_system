ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About us

Helping connect students and accommodation providers near Mulungushi University.

Our aim

The Off-Campus Accommodation System is intended to make it easier for Mulungushi University students in Kabwe to discover accommodation and communicate with landlords.

Designed for a shared community

The planned service includes student, landlord, and administrator areas. Students will be able to find and request rooms; landlords will manage houses and respond to residents; administrators will review landlord accounts and manage user roles.

Current version

This version focuses on page structure and navigation. It is a local browser prototype, not a live accommodation or account service.
