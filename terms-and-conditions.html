Demonstration only

This website is a prototype for an off-campus accommodation system. It is not a live booking service and does not create a tenancy or guarantee accommodation.

Demo account information

Student and landlord demo accounts are saved in browser storage on the device where they are created. Do not enter real passwords, sensitive personal data, or confidential information. Browser storage is not protected as a production account system.

Listings and decisions

Any future example listings or workflow screens must be independently verified before anyone relies on them. The current prototype does not submit bookings, notices, complaints, approvals, or role changes.
